# Symmetry-Augmented Sparse Recovery

Status: `full_research`

## Claim

Known group actions can improve sparse dictionary recovery in sample-limited
settings, but only when the augmentation is the correct symmetry. The headline
metric is strict one-to-one feature recovery, not loose best-match recovery.

## Research Question

When observations are generated by sparse combinations of features arranged in
cyclic orbits, does unlabeled cyclic augmentation recover more true features
than the same number of raw observations, and does that improvement survive a
false-symmetry control?

## Current Evidence

Command:

```bash
python experiments/symmetry-sparse-recovery/symmetry_augmented_sparse_recovery.py --output artifacts/hard_push_symmetry_sparse_with_control.csv
```

Result summary:

| samples | method | unique cosine | strict recovery at 0.90 | orbit closure | mse |
| ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 40 | baseline | 0.410 | 0.373 | 0.339 | 0.0917 |
| 40 | cyclic_augmented | 0.506 | 0.486 | 0.489 | 0.0958 |
| 40 | shuffled_augmented | 0.471 | 0.033 | 0.553 | 0.1762 |
| 70 | baseline | 0.464 | 0.448 | 0.414 | 0.0930 |
| 70 | cyclic_augmented | 0.529 | 0.509 | 0.512 | 0.0919 |
| 70 | shuffled_augmented | 0.492 | 0.030 | 0.590 | 0.1761 |
| 120 | baseline | 0.491 | 0.488 | 0.441 | 0.0927 |
| 120 | cyclic_augmented | 0.532 | 0.510 | 0.517 | 0.0899 |
| 120 | shuffled_augmented | 0.488 | 0.031 | 0.588 | 0.1797 |

The false-symmetry control is the key upgrade. Shuffled coordinate augmentation
has the same row multiplier as cyclic augmentation but nearly zero strict
0.90 recovery and much worse reconstruction error. The positive result is not
just "NMF saw more rows."

## Baselines And Controls

- `baseline`: raw sample-limited observations.
- `cyclic_augmented`: every known cyclic transform of each observation.
- `shuffled_augmented`: size-matched false action using random coordinate
  permutations.
- `unique_feature_recovery`: one-to-one assignment metric using Hungarian
  matching.
- `feature_recovery`: loose best-match diagnostic only.

## Why This Survives

This is still synthetic, but it now has the shape of a real research artifact:
a falsifiable mechanism, a strict metric, a positive result, and a negative
control that kills the boring explanation.

## Failure Conditions

Give up on this track if the cyclic advantage disappears when moving from
handmade nonnegative dictionaries to controlled activation-like data with known
or estimated group actions.

Do not claim anything about LLM sparse autoencoders until the experiment touches
real or realistically simulated activation spaces.

## Next Full Experiment

Build a controlled activation-space fixture: take representations with a known
cyclic transform, mix them through a fixed nonnegative observation matrix, and
repeat the strict-recovery plus false-symmetry comparison. If the cyclic lift
survives that bridge, this becomes worth testing on real model activations.
